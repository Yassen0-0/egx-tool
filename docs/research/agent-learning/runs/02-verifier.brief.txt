<task>
The workspace contains note.jpg, a photo of a handwritten notebook page (mostly Egyptian Arabic, a few English words), and reader.md, another agent's reading of that page. You are the verifier. Your job is to confirm or correct that reading. This is a read-only job: do not create, edit or delete any file, do not run git, do not search the web.

Work in this order, and do not skip step 1:
1. Open note.jpg and read the page yourself, element by element, BEFORE opening reader.md. Write down your own transcription first (it goes in section 1 of your report). If you cannot view the image pixels, reply with the single line CANNOT_SEE_IMAGE and stop.
2. Only then open reader.md and compare it with your reading, element by element: the text of every element, every arrow and its direction, and the interpretation.
3. For every disagreement, go back to the image, zoom into that region as far as you can, and decide which reading is right, or say that the image cannot settle it.

Do not agree just because the other reading sounds plausible. Look for: missed elements, misread words, arrows in the wrong direction, and interpretations that add ideas not on the page.
</task>

<research_mode>
Keep observed, inferred and open strictly apart. Never present an inference as something written on the page.
</research_mode>

1. قراءتي المستقلة
   A numbered list, one item per element: مكانه، نوعه، النص بالظبط، ودرجة الثقة (عالية / متوسطة / منخفضة).

2. جدول المقارنة
   One row per element: العنصر | قراءة reader.md | قراءتي | الحكم (متفق / مختلف / مش واضح في الصورة) | السبب.

3. الأسهم والشبكة
   Do the arrows and boxes in reader.md match the image? List every difference.

4. مراجعة التفسير
   Which parts of reader.md's interpretation are supported by the page, which are additions, and what it missed.

5. الحكم النهائي
   One line: «القراءة صحيحة»، أو «صحيحة مع تصحيحات»، أو «فيها أخطاء جوهرية». After it, the corrected final transcription.
